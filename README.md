# Botfit — Website Design & Development

A complete front-end design and build for Botfit, an indoor family entertainment centre in Gurugram — designed and developed end-to-end as part of my internship at Medkon.

## About the Project

Botfit needed a website revamp to replace an outdated, unprofessional-looking site with a design that felt polished and trustworthy enough to convert visitors into party and event bookings, while keeping the brand's playful, family-first energy.

## My Role

Working within an information architecture and brand color/typography system provided by Medkon, I owned the complete design-to-development execution:

- Designed all UI screens and page layouts (Home, About, Activities, Birthday Parties, Corporate Events, School Trips, BotCafe, and supporting pages)
- Built custom interactions and micro-animations across the site
- Developed the full front-end using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript
- Structured and coded every page from design to a fully functioning, responsive site

## Pages Included

- Home / Landing
- About Botfit
- Activities
- Birthday Parties
- Corporate Events
- School Trips
- BotCafe
- Plan Your Visit
- Contact / Thank You, Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions
