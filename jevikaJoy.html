Our Mission

India's children deserve
their own heroes.

Every culture has characters children
look up to. For decades, Indian children
grew up with heroes borrowed from other
markets — different values, different
bodies, different worlds.

Jevika and Joy were created to change
that. They look like Indian kids, move
like Indian kids, and represent the
values Indian families care about —
teamwork, effort, courage, and joy.

They are not a marketing gimmick. They
are a long-term commitment to building
an Indian physical activity culture from
the ground up.
