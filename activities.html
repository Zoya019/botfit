FAQS

Everything you
need to know.

Botfit has activities for every age from 3 years to adults. Each activity card on this page shows its recommended age range. Our staff will guide your child to the right zones at check-in.
Walk-ins are welcome during general opening hours. For birthday parties, corporate events, or private group bookings, advance booking is strongly recommended to guarantee zone availability.
Parents are welcome to accompany children in the younger activity zones. For most activities, children are supervised by our staff so parents can relax in the café or viewing areas.
Comfortable sports clothing is recommended. Grip socks are mandatory for trampoline and climbing activities — available for purchase at the venue if needed. No open footwear.
