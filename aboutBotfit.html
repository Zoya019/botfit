The Beginning

A city that needed
something different.

Gurugram had malls, screens, and
soft play corners. What it didn't have was a
serious, premium space where children could
move, compete, and grow — and where parents
could actually relax.

Botfit was founded to fill that gap.
We built North India's largest indoor family
entertainment centre with real activities,
real supervision, and a real philosophy —
movement first, screens never.

Today, over 10,000 families call
Botfit their go-to for birthdays, school
trips, team outings, and weekend adventures.

Our Philosophy

Screen-free.
Movement-first.
Emotionally safe.

Every decision at Botfit — from activity
design to staff training — comes back to
three principles.

01
Screen-Free
No tablets. No screens. No passive entertainment. Every activity at Botfit requires your child to move, think, and engage with other humans.
02
Movement-First
Physical activity is not a reward — it's the entire point. We designed 20+ activities that build strength, coordination, confidence, and team spirit.
03
Emotionally Safe
Our staff are trained to include every child — the shy one, the nervous one, the one who needs a moment. No child is left on the sidelines at Botfit.
