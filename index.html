Safety & Hygiene

Every detail.
Every child.
Every time.

We treat safety as the baseline, not a feature. From the moment a child walks in to the moment they leave, every process — physical, hygienic, and operational — is designed around their wellbeing and your peace of mind.

01
Trained Event Co-ordinators
Every event — birthday, corporate, school trip — is managed by a dedicated co-ordinator who is trained in child supervision, crowd management, and first-response basics. No freelancers. No understaffed days.
02
Safe, Managed Activity Environment
All equipment is certified, load-tested, and inspected before every session. Activity zones are capacity-controlled, with trained floor staff maintaining safe participant ratios at all times.
03
Cleanliness & Hygiene Standards
Every play surface, soft structure, and high-contact zone is sanitised between sessions. Restrooms are cleaned continuously. Air quality and ventilation are monitored throughout operating hours.
04
Child-Friendly Food Handling
BotCafe maintains strict allergen protocols and hygienic food prep standards. All meals are prepared fresh, with no cross-contamination risks — particularly for common allergies in younger children.
05
Parent Comfort & Supervision Support
Clearly designated parent zones with full sightlines to activity areas. Our staff keeps parents informed and at ease — you're always close, but never in the way of a child discovering independence.
06
Entry & Exit Assistance
Controlled check-in with wristband protocols ensures no child leaves without a verified parent or guardian. Entry and exit points are staffed at all times during events and open-play sessions.

Certified equipment. Trained hosts. Zero shortcuts. — These aren't claims. They're the operational standards every Botfit session is held to, every single day.
