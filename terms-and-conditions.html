Botfit reserves the right to refuse any booking at its sole discretion.

Non-refundable deposit at booking. Balance payable after event ends, on actual headcount or minimum — whichever is higher.
Corporate Events
50% advance

School Trips

Venue & Play Area Rules

For the safety, hygiene, and enjoyment of all guests:

8.1 No shoes in the play area. All footwear must be removed at the entrance to the activity zone.
8.2 No eatables, drinks, chewing gum, or candies inside the play area — hygiene and choking hazard prevention.
8.3 No personal items such as toys, sharp objects, jewellery, or watches in or around the play area.
8.4 Children with injuries, illness, fever, or contagious conditions are not advised to use the play area. Entry is at the parent's risk.
8.5 Age restrictions apply to certain zones. Guests must follow zone signage and staff instructions at all times.
8.6 Guests may relax in the cafeteria area for an extended time. Smoking is only permitted in the designated outdoor area.
8.7 Alcohol is permitted only with Botfit's prior written permission. Unauthorised alcohol will be confiscated.
8.8 Pets are permitted (leashed at all times). Owners are fully responsible for behaviour and hygiene. Pets are not allowed in the play area or cafeteria food zones.

Guests must follow all instructions from Botfit staff and posted signage at all times.
